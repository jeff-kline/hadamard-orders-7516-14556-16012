# When the matrices were recorded

All local times below are Central Standard Time, UTC−6, in Chicago. The three November 25 times are the last-write times of the original uncompressed matrix files, before copying them into this draft. Each original was hashed again against the digest in the matrix certificate table. They establish that those exact bytes were present by those morning times. The author recalls finding the constructions that morning. A local file time does not establish a first public disclosure or the instant a proof was conceived. [Matrix digests](CERTIFICATES.md)
