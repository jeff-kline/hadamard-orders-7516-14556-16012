# Earlier construction routes for 14556, 58228 and 80092

As of 25 November 2026, this search found no verified independent earlier construction at any of these three orders. The published ordinary-order table of Egan, Kotsireas, London and Ó Catháin stops at 10000. Its omission of these orders carries no evidence of priority. [Survey, section 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.27175v2#S4)

## Order 14556

Since 14556 = 4 × 3 × 1213, the Cooper–Wallis construction with length-3 T-sequences would suffice given Williamson-type matrices of order 1213. Seberry and Yamada explicitly list **two** conditional ways to supply those matrices. One uses a symmetric conference matrix of order 606. The other uses Williamson-type matrices of order 303. A general Hadamard matrix of order 1212 does not by itself furnish the second input. For the first route the normalized conference core would give a strongly regular graph with parameters (605,302,150,151). Brouwer's parameter table marks that row with a question mark. The broader Miyamoto route allows a symmetric quaternary Hadamard matrix of order 606. I found no verified supply for any of these inputs. [Seberry and Yamada, Corollary 29 and pp. 120–121](https://combinatorialpress.com/article/jcmcc/Volume%2007/vol-007-paper%206.pdf) · [Brouwer's table](https://aeb.win.tue.nl/graphs/srg/srgtab601-650.html) · [Symmetry condition, Remark 8](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.27175v2#S4)

Yamada's older theorem gives another precise test. The prime 3637 is 5 modulo 8, so a **skew** Hadamard matrix of order (3637+3)/2 = 1820 would already imply 14556. The ordinary order 1820 is insufficient. Seberry and Yamada's historical skew table gives exponent 4 at odd part 455, whereas 1820 requires exponent 2. I found no later skew witness of order 1820. The older ordinary benchmark 29112 follows from Williamson-type matrices of order 101, Cooper–Wallis at length 3, Miyamoto at 1213 and the Agaian product with order 12. [Yamada, second construction](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-the-australian-mathematical-society/article/some-new-series-of-hadamard-matrices/2CF4B3D949273D45717E6F5FED183827) · [Historical skew table 7.1](https://documents.uow.edu.au/~jennie/WEBPDF/175_1992.pdf) · [Williamson input of order 101](https://combinatorialpress.com/article/jcmcc/Volume%2007/vol-007-paper%206.pdf) · [Product formulas, Table 4](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.27175v2#S4)

## Orders 58228 and 80092

For 58228 the odd part 14557 is prime and 1 modulo 4. Miyamoto gives 4 × 14557 from the order-14556 matrix. A prior construction of 14556 would therefore settle both priority comparisons. Direct Paley I and II fail the prime-power test at 58227 = 3 × 13 × 1493 and 29113 = 7 × 4159 respectively. A different route to Williamson-type matrices at 14557 would use a symmetric quaternary Hadamard matrix of order 7278. I found no verified earlier input at that order. [Miyamoto](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/0097316591900085) · [Table 4 and Remark 8](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.27175v2#S4)

For 80092 the odd part 20023 is prime and 3 modulo 4, outside Miyamoto's ordinary 4q case. The Paley field candidates 80091 = 9 × 11 × 809 and 40045 = 5 × 8009 both fail. Yamada's second construction applies at prime 20021, which is 5 modulo 8, **if a skew Hadamard matrix of order 10012 exists**. A source for that input would independently establish the target. Karoui supplies the skew order 1252 used by the present construction. I found no source carrying that input to skew order 10012. The two prime odd parts 14557 and 20023 also rule out a Cooper–Wallis factorization with both factors greater than one. These checks leave general difference-family and other sporadic constructions unresolved.
